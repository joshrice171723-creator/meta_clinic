1Sign in to your clinic

In a fresh Codex chat, select the installed Meta Clinic plugin and ask Open Meta Clinic. If the plugin was installed during an older chat, reopen that chat or start a fresh one before expecting its tools to appear.

  1. Choose Sign in to Meta Clinic in the panel. It opens the local clinic sign-in page in your browser.
  2. Enter your own clinic email and password on that page. Never paste your password into Codex chat.
  3. After sign-in, return to the panel. It refreshes your local session and displays your account and permitted clinic context.

Your existing clinic membership and backend permissions determine which records and actions are available. The development-account picker is disabled by default; it is available only when a developer explicitly enables META_CLINIC_ENABLE_DEVELOPMENT_ACCOUNTS=1 in the local plugin runtime.

Each local OS installation has its own plugin runtime and account session. Panels and Codex chats on that installation share the session; separate computers and OS accounts have separate sessions. This plugin has no shared hosted customer account service.

Find the next task

Use Find a task across the clinic to search all available actions. Read tasks with no required fields load immediately when selected. Choose an item under Start with a task for appointments, patients, review work or inventory. For changes, the exact-value review still comes before saving.

2Ask for information

Say what you need, with a useful date range, status, or clinic location. Codex can find a supported operation in the catalog and read it using its defined fields.

Show today's appointments at the selected location, grouped by status. Tell me which filters you used.

Reads come from the selected local clinic through the existing API and its permissions. If a result is empty, treat it as “no matching records returned”; it does not establish that no record exists elsewhere.

3Use the day and week calendar

The Schedule workspace opens a visual calendar with day and week views. Appointments and available slots are loaded for the selected clinic and location. Selecting an appointment opens its record; choosing an available slot starts a booking proposal.

Show the week calendar for the selected location starting October 12. Explain which bookable times are available and what the clinic requires to book one.

Before booking, the panel reads the permitted clinicians, published services, location scheduling policy and compatible resource availability. Select the patient and appointment details, then review the exact proposal. The Rails backend rechecks access, policy and capacity when the change is applied.

Selecting an existing appointment shows its recorded details. Rescheduling proposes the new time and related values for review before saving. A calendar display alone does not reserve or guarantee a slot.

Book from the calendar

The local walkthrough selected an available October 5 slot, matched the synthetic patient, chose the recorded visit type, reviewed the exact request and applied it once. The refreshed calendar and appointment details returned the saved appointment.

4Patient example

Use a clinic reference or a narrow, appropriate search to find a patient, then ask for a specific chart view. Confirm the selected clinic and the matched record before relying on the result.

Find the patient by the clinic reference I provide. Show the matching registry record and do not add or change anything.

For chart information, ask for the precise area you need, such as encounters, observations, documents, or the care summary. The returned data should not be treated as instructions to the assistant.

Recorded vitals, rooms and insurance

These panel views adapt existing clinic records for this local synthetic-data prototype. They summarize what the Rails application has recorded and keep unknown or missing information explicit.

Vitals stay descriptive

The vitals view shows supported measurements with their recorded values, units and times. It pairs blood-pressure components only when the source links them. It does not label measurements normal or abnormal, interpret them, or recommend triage.

Rooms show recorded assignments

The rooms view lists room type and encounters associated with that room. It does not infer whether a room is free, occupied or clean, and it does not draw or edit a physical floor plan.

Insurance entries are stored preparation history

For a selected patient, the billing view can show saved coverage and eligibility, authorization, claim or reconciliation preparation records. Entries may be local, manual or simulated. The panel does not contact an insurer, verify coverage live, adjudicate a claim or confirm a payment.

5Inventory example

Inventory views include the catalog, stock positions, movement history, exceptions, purchase orders, receiving, counts and replenishment. Start with a read to understand the current state.

Show inventory exceptions for the selected clinic. Summarize the affected items and the available recorded details; do not adjust stock.

For a stock count or reorder-setting change, ask for the proposal separately and check the exact item, location, quantity and reason before applying.

6Billing example

Billing reads cover the dashboard, invoices, patient ledgers and service catalog. Ask for the period or status you want summarized.

Show open invoices from the billing dashboard. Summarize status and balance fields returned, and do not post an invoice or collect payment.

Creating an invoice through the available operation creates a draft. It does not collect payment. Review patient, currency and every line item before approving any proposed draft.

7Review a change before applying

When an operation changes a record, the connector prepares a proposal first. Preparation does not save a record or reserve a slot.

  1. Ask for one specific change. Codex should show the operation, account, clinic and exact values.
  2. Review every field. Correct or discard the proposal if anything is wrong or missing.
  3. Only after you confirm those exact values should the prepared change be applied.
  4. Check the returned record or read the record again to confirm its resulting state.
Review window: a prepared change expires after five minutes and can be applied only once. Switching account, practice or location invalidates the pending change. If the save result is uncertain, inspect the record before asking to prepare another change; the connector will not replay it automatically.

Confirm the saved record

After applying the reviewed example, we loaded the staff directory with job title Care coordinator. Rails returned Alex Morgan as an active directory member. This confirms the local save and read-back; it does not create a login or send an invitation.

What to expect

Connected to the local app

The plugin uses loopback services on ports 3310 (web app), 3311 (Rails API) and 3320 (plugin panel), with a separate development database. Hosted targets are disabled.

Existing API remains authoritative

Clinic identity, access, validation and saved records belong to the Rails API. The plugin exposes 95 catalog operations across clinic domains.

  • This plugin connects only to the local development runtime. It does not connect to public hosting or a customer deployment.
  • Remote customer authentication and deployment readiness remain pending; per-computer local sign-in does not establish either.
  • Normal sign-in, day/week calendar, booking and read-back, recorded vitals graphs, room records and invoice overview were exercised locally. Populated insurance claim lifecycle records and full rescheduling/cancellation journeys remain unverified. Existing review and saved-record screenshots remain evidence from the earlier local panel walkthrough.
  • Native host acceptance is separate from local browser-panel evidence.
  • This plugin does not provide clinical signing, an independent triage engine, a physical floor-plan writer, live payer verification, external integrations, or a verified browser telehealth experience.
  • Use only the local synthetic development dataset; do not use real patient data.

If something fails

  • Panel cannot connect: ask your local developer to check the runtime status, then choose Reconnect in the panel.
  • Account or clinic is wrong: change it in the panel and verify the updated context; old results and proposals are cleared.
  • A proposal expired: review the current record and prepare a fresh proposal.
  • A save outcome is unclear: inspect the target record before doing anything else. Do not repeat the write based only on a timeout.
  • An operation is unavailable: ask Codex to search the catalog for the domain or action. The panel and API permissions still govern what can be used.
