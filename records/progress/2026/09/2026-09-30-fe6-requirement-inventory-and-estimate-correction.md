## Status labels

- **Source-present:** a specified bounded mechanism has implementation and a consuming path in source. This is the source-complete label for that bounded contract only; it is not whole-milestone completion or executed proof.
- **Partial:** an implemented subset leaves named broader capabilities absent or limited.
- **Missing:** a named mechanism is absent at fe6. If selecting its detailed contract is still necessary, that uncertainty is stated separately rather than invented.
- **Undefined/external:** scope, policy, provider mapping or professional/operating acceptance is not selected or not evidenced. It is neither implemented nor automatically a ready source assignment.
- **Runtime-unverified:** this combined candidate has no new runtime/schema/test/build verification. This independent label applies to **every row below**, including source-present mechanisms. It does not itself withhold Build credit or make tests a Build criterion. Known broken source cannot be called implemented.
