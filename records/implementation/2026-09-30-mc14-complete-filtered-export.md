## Technical limits and explicit failures

- At most **10,000 combined detail rows per issuance**, including both payment/refund or pharmacy/office sections. Counts are checked before loading full detail, then independently reconciled before persistence.
- At most **2,000,000 UTF-8 CSV bytes** and **4,000,000 bytes of canonical source/provenance JSON**. Exceeding either fails before receipt creation. The source envelope cap includes the full saved projection and provenance metadata, not only detail rows.
- **15 seconds cooperative source-capture deadline**, checked around counts/materialization, within detail conversion and invoice currency summaries, and at capture completion. **5 seconds per SQL statement** and **5 seconds database lock wait**, transaction-local to issuance. Deadline checks do not interrupt an already executing statement; SQL timeout bounds those statements, and the next check rejects overdue capture. These are not a hard end-to-end wall-clock SLA. Serialization operates on the already row/byte-bounded captured data.
- Resource/count failures roll back creation and issue no artifact or receipt; users must narrow available report filters or retry explicitly when a transient time limit is involved. No new durable job queue, public sharing/storage provider, retention duration, legal-hold policy, new grants, live data, or provider/payment call is introduced. Registered resettable lesson clinics retain their existing issuance exclusion.

The largest allowed row count is a technical ceiling, not a promise every 10,000-row report will fit its byte/time ceilings. A complete artifact is issued only if all checks succeed. Zero matching rows still produce truthful zero counts and complete empty detail sections. Broader report enrollment, administrator-wide history and clinical/retention/operational acceptance remain outside this task.
