# Weight-management intake audit — 2026-09-19

Baseline: `a0465dea4a25d4915eceb22b748d2c6c5404290d`, local synthetic environment. Scope: M1–M12 against the full user-supplied adult nonsurgical intake brief, including its 2026-09-19 attachment. This record supersedes the earlier claim that all twelve milestones were complete. It does not change or reduce their original acceptance criteria.

## Conclusion

**Not all requested work is done.** The existing guided shell, answer states, repeating histories, observation entry and handoff records are useful foundations. Several later milestones are represented by summary/status selections rather than the detailed records and connected workflows specified in the brief. An enum such as `reviewed`, `completed`, `confirmed`, or `clinician` is not evidence of a clinical review, scored instrument, signed consent or assigned clarification task.

The audit inspected `PrevisitIntake`, `NurseWorkflow`, `NurseEncounterWorkspace`, their direct tests and the milestone definitions. Missing below means missing from, or not connected/proven in, this intake journey; it does not mean that no owning domain elsewhere in the application implements the underlying capability. Reuse those domains instead of building competing medication, allergy, consent, document or task stores.

## Confirmed defects hardened in this pass

1. **M1, high:** raw `answered` with whitespace could confirm a group, then normalize to unanswered. Validation now uses normalized answers.
2. **M1/M11, high:** unchanged confirmed-index arrays skipped all answer validation. Retained confirmations are revalidated, changes reopen the affected and later groups, and draft recovery can reduce the confirmed prefix. Out-of-order/out-of-range/unconfirmed advancement remains rejected.
3. **M1, medium:** group zero was reused as an automatic-resume sentinel, preventing a deliberate return to the first group. Resume now happens on version hydration; selecting group one stays there. Saved versions also prevent an older resource snapshot overwriting a just-saved revision.
4. **M3, medium:** adding later groups moved clinical measurement entry from Measurements to the final Follow-up group. Placement now follows the stable group ID.
5. **M4–M6/M11, high:** rendered summaries omitted stored reactions, complications, clinician details, adverse effects, stop reasons and dose certainty. These are now included for chart readers.
6. **M7, high:** breastfeeding, pregnancy plans and contraception disappeared when pregnancy status was declined/unanswered. They are independent questions with explicit answer states.
7. **M1/M12, medium:** initial `habits` and follow-up `changes`, `history_changes`, `change_details`, `habits` had no group and were unreachable in the nurse flow. They now have server-owned mappings; a coverage test checks every specialty field.
8. **M1, medium:** explicit zero/negative/false/null schema versions could be accepted. Only supported positive versions are accepted; absent legacy versions remain readable.
9. **M11, high:** a handoff could request an old intake or accept an intake superseded after request. Request and accept/complete now check the latest revision under the encounter lock. Cancellation and a new handoff remain available; the UI identifies the stale handoff. Room presence is rechecked under that lock at request time.
10. **UI, medium:** every group displayed the entire saved narrative before its current questions. An accessible Material UI accordion now keeps the saved summary closed by default. Group chips visibly say Confirmed and locked groups are disabled.
11. **M2, medium:** frontend readiness required a physical room even for video encounters. It now follows the existing video exemption.
12. **M2, medium, found in browser:** the demographics table labeled the preferred email as Phone, despite an active phone in the profile. The chart now selects only active phone contacts, preferring the preferred phone rather than any preferred contact; a focused test covers email and retired-phone exclusion.
13. **UI, low, found in browser:** labels repeated “status status,” help omitted Not asked, and current symptoms were described as “previous current symptoms.” Shared intake copy is corrected for both staff and patient surfaces.
