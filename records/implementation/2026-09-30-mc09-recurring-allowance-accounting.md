## Explicit contract

A selected service component may retain this optional `allowance` object:

```json
{"anchor":"paid_period_start","timezone":"UTC","reset":"discard_unused","renewal":"verified_paid_period","partial_window":"full_quantity"}
```

All five values must be deliberately configured. Missing/null policy remains valid, explicitly unconfigured accounting; partial, extra or unsupported choices prevent publication/preview. The existing catalog draft/write, stale-revision checks and separate publication authority are retained. Each choice has an initially unconfigured catalog select; a deliberate clear action removes the policy. New published versions affect new purchases only. Purchased `selected_components` remain the authority; catalog edits never recalculate earlier balances. Existing catalog templates do not acquire policy automatically.

Supported frequency mechanics are day (24 hours), week (7 days), month (anchor day and UTC time, clamped to the last day in shorter months without drifting), and term (one retained paid period). Window start is included; end is excluded and clipped to the recorded paid end. The fifth choice explicitly allows the full configured quantity in a short final window. There is no inferred proration. A new verified paid period restarts the allowance only when that policy was purchased. Unused and reversed units never carry into another window.

Only an existing retained `active` agreement event whose recorded start/end contains the current business clock supplies a paid window. Absence of such an event, expired access, failed renewal without a new active event, or overlapping paid windows supplies no new usable allowance. Existing agreement state/cancellation continues to gate new use. Paid-through cancellation permits use only while existing access is active. No provider handler, invoice, charge or payment behavior changes. Reads derive grants without writing ledger entries.

Recurring use requires positive whole units up to remaining allowance and the exact current token. A stale new request is rejected rather than applied to a new window. Keys cannot be reused across the fixed and recurring ledgers. Same-transaction audit failure rolls back the evidence.

Responses add `entry.ledger` (`fixed`/`recurring`) and `entry.allowance_window` (null for fixed entries). Recurring balances use `source: retained_recurring_allowance`, window timestamps/token, and `allowance_unconfigured` or `paid_period_unavailable` when no usable policy/window exists.
