    04 / How to read the map

    Evidence has edges

    Source exists

    Implemented fields, routes and guards can be present without a complete patient or staff journey. The build bar describes this dimension only.

    Local path proved

    A focused check or synthetic browser path proves only the recorded action and environment. Reload, denial and exception paths may still be open.

    External gate remains

    Vendor acceptance, clinical decisions, operational ownership and release approval need their own evidence. Local simulation does not grant them.
