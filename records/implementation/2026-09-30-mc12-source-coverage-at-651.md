## Durable reload private working-copy gap, precisely

P2 explicitly says to keep the component mounted during the interrupted POST and retry; **after acknowledged retry**, reload and GET must retain the saved request. It also explicitly says: “Do not claim safe retry-key retention across reload/navigation.” P5–P7 require reading persisted request/appointment and prior work; they do not require preserving a private pre-acknowledgment form across a full reload. OP-07 requires truthful uncertainty and safe reconciliation; it does not select a storage architecture. The historical proposal's generic reload/recovery evidence is not an accepted requirement to store sensitive forms. Thus durable private working-copy storage is an absent capability, but is not an independently established accepted MC12 build requirement here.

Current limits are concrete: mounted billing/new-booking owners preserve exact attempt key/body; full page reload or component unmount destroys those references. Existing request tables persist only submitted intent. Recent message/refill lists (30) and appointment requests (20) are not an exact attempt lookup protocol; absence cannot disprove an in-flight submission, and there is no retained original key/body with which to replay. An acknowledged submission survives reload through its owning GET. No claim of pre-acknowledgment reload safety is made.

Existing conventions already settle much of the storage question:

- [Web instructions](../../apps/web/AGENTS.md) prohibit records, signing content, passwords, CSRF material and protected values in local/session storage, require reauthorization after restore and clearing protected state on logout/account change. No sensitive browser persistence is a permissible shortcut.
- [WorkingDraft](../../apps/api/app/models/clinic/working_draft.rb), [WorkingDraftsController](../../apps/api/app/controllers/clinic/working_drafts_controller.rb) and [useWorkingDraft](../../apps/web/src/features/clinic/useWorkingDraft.ts) supply encrypted, private, versioned **staff** working copies scoped to practice, actor membership and context. Kind-specific staff capabilities, patient/encounter/site authorization, expected-version/exact-write reconciliation and content-free audit references already exist. They do not authorize patient access; patient accounts must not be granted staff memberships/capabilities to reuse them.
- Patient intake already has encrypted versioned, patient-authorized saved drafts. Those are official intake revisions with their own appointment/form/attestation semantics; using them to hold billing messages or booking intents would change their meaning. Submitted requests and signed clinical/financial history remain distinct from mutable private copies.

No further policy is needed to satisfy P2's selected mounted-retry plus acknowledged-reload boundary. If the product owner explicitly selects stronger pre-acknowledgment reload recovery, it needs a narrow contract: which fields/operations are retained; practice/patient/identity ownership; behavior after identity revocation/account switch; expiration/deletion and submitted-record handoff; exact-key/body retrieval and reconciliation of uncertain private-copy writes; explicit resumption without automatic clinical/booking submission. The privacy/security owner would approve that retention/access/data-flow boundary using existing encrypted server conventions, not invent clinical release or communication rules. A new patient-authorized server route/schema may then be necessary; no new staff grants, browser protected storage, blanket security hold or live provider action is implied. This clarifies the prior [return evidence](2026-09-30-mc12-return-request-recovery.md): its “future increment needs” list was conditional architecture work, not an accepted requirement or proof that MC12 source cannot finish without it.

## Unambiguous remaining local source gaps

These are concrete current controls that conflict with OP-07/10 or the delivered same-action retry contract. They are not requests to expand clinical policy or generic “needs acceptance” statements. No repair is included in this docs-only commit.

1. **Patient refill attempt can be replaced while unknown.** PatientRefillRequests lines 43–54 choose a new key when medication/pharmacy/note changes; lines 124–164 disable fields only while busy. After a potentially committed response is lost, editing then submitting overwrites the retained attempt and sends a new request before resolving the first. Its unknown classifier also requires ApiError, so an ordinary network Error does not get the explicit unknown warning. Finishline: retain and lock the captured request through uncertainty, including transport errors and subsequent rejection, allow explicit exact retry, and validate acknowledgment without changing clinical review or staff delivery.
2. **Existing reschedule/cancel dialog unlocks prior uncertainty.** PatientAppointmentRequests lines 803–817 assigns `outcomeUnconfirmed` from only the latest error, omits 408, and clears it on a later 4xx after an earlier possible commit. Reschedule fields then become editable and mint a new key (lines 847–874); cancel can close/reopen and mint a fresh key. Finishline: distinguish initial known rejection from a rejection after unknown, preserve original source snapshot/key/body, serialize explicit retry and verify matching acknowledgment. The repaired new-booking helper does not govern this dialog.
3. **Waitlist reply can discard unresolved receipt identity.** PatientWaitlistOffers lines 59–89 treats ApiError 408 and explicit `outcome_unconfirmed` below 500 as known rejection and clears `attempt`; a later 4xx also replaces prior unknown. This undermines its already-delivered immutable exact-reply receipt contract. Finishline: preserve original entry/action/version/key until authoritative exact acknowledgment or reconciliation proves its outcome; a changed offer/eligibility rejection alone cannot disprove earlier commit. Existing decline-status refresh must remain truthful about what it establishes.
4. **Intake save classifies 408 as definitive and can clear a prior unknown attempt.** PatientPrevisitNote lines 357–373 clears its attempt for ordinary 4xx, including 408; a later rejected exact retry can similarly discard earlier uncertainty. Finishline: preserve captured form/body/version/key after possible commit and reconcile owning saved revision before allowing a changed save. Its explicit genuine stale-version workflow must continue preserving local draft and source history. This does not authorize broader private-form storage.

Other visible caps are recorded above and in the [shared paging evidence](2026-09-30-shared-work-inbox-paging.md). Latest-N portal lists are not represented as full archives; no accepted full-history requirement was found that makes every such cap a new MC12 source obligation. Ordinary ClinicalTask previews and unrelated callback/refill/appointment work populations were not made fully paginated by this increment. No patient-visible raw encounter-follow-up or lab-results release contract was found; absence is not permission to expose private clinical source.
