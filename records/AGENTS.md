# Historical-record instructions

These instructions apply to `records/` and extend the repository contract.

- Records preserve dated evidence outside the default agent/developer context. They do not own current status, authority, requirements or implementation instructions.
- Preserve source/task/decision IDs, event dates, failures, limits and original scope. Do not silently rewrite a historical claim to match current behavior.
- Prefer append-only corrections or an explicit superseding record. Never discard incident, recovery, migration, security or professional-review evidence merely to reduce size.
- New indexes and navigation use repository-relative Markdown links. Archived snapshots may retain their original vault wiki links as evidence of their source context.
- Keep patient information, confidential applicant material and credentials out of records. Use synthetic examples only.
