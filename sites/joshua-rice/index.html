Meta Clinic

One foundation.
Different ways to deliver care.

A healthcare platform I am building independently

A shared foundation that can be configured for the way a clinic delivers care.

Every clinic begins with one core platform. Its capabilities can be configured for a care model—such as weight loss, infusion, or telehealth—without rebuilding the foundation.

Meta Clinic is designed to bring patient acquisition and marketing touchpoints, intake, scheduling, documentation, billing, memberships, medication, inventory, and telehealth into one workspace. That gives a local clinic the infrastructure to serve patients nearby and grow into a broader provider.

My work spans the Rails API, React interface, PostgreSQL data model, role-based access, tenant isolation, and audit history. The guided demo shows how those pieces support a connected clinic journey.

Engineering for healthcare

I understand HIPAA’s privacy and security principles and the responsibility that comes with handling protected health information. That knowledge shapes the questions I bring to healthcare software:

Who can access this?
Think through authentication, role-based permissions, and appropriate access to patient information.
Can we account for what happened?
Consider access auditing, change history, and the integrity of important records.
Where does sensitive information go?
Consider data protection in storage and transit, logs, exports, and third-party integrations.

I approach compliance as shared work across engineering, security, operations, and policy.
