Book with the assistant

The booking journey below was verified locally with a real AI response and a saved appointment. Broader workflow support is under construction. This is not a hosted release.

1. Open the assistant

Enter the demo and choose a clinic role. You do not need to log in or create an account. In the clinic workspace, select Assistant. Staff use their existing clinic session.

The panel follows your current clinic, location and patient. Check the displayed names before beginning. Changing the patient, clinic, location or role invalidates any pending proposal.

2. Find appointment openings

Select the patient, visit type and clinician, then ask for a day and time or choose a date. For example: “Find me an appointment tomorrow at 9:30 for 30 minutes.” The returned openings come from the clinic schedule. An opening is not reserved until your booking succeeds.

If no openings match, choose another date or clinician. When natural-language assistance is unavailable, the direct scheduling controls remain available under the same permissions.

3. Review and confirm

Select an opening to prepare a proposal. Review the patient, location, clinician, visit type and time. Only the confirmation button can submit that proposal. A message such as “yes” does not book an appointment.

Proposals expire and the schedule can change. If the proposal is stale, search again and review a fresh proposal. The assistant will not silently substitute another time.

4. Check the saved appointment

A successful confirmation displays the appointment read back from the clinic records and its action receipt. Use the schedule link to see the persisted appointment. Repeating confirmation must not create a second appointment.

When something needs attention

  • Permission denied: your selected role cannot perform that action. In the demo, choose another available role through the demo controls; start a new proposal afterward.
  • Availability changed: refresh openings and review a new proposal.
  • Confirmation outcome unknown: check the saved result before attempting another booking. Do not assume a network error means nothing was saved.
  • Daily limit reached: natural-language assistance is paused. Use the direct scheduling controls or try again another day.
  • Context changed or session expired: reopen the workspace context. Old proposals cannot be confirmed under a different identity or role.

What this milestone covers

Appointment openings, reviewed booking and persisted confirmation are the first milestone. Clinical drafts, billing actions, standing briefings and automatic actions remain unavailable. The assistant cannot sign clinical notes, grant permissions or override clinic policy.

Conversation text stays in the current browser session's memory and is cleared with a context or account change. It is not a clinical record. Action receipts are stored separately by the backend.
