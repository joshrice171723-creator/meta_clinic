## Top risks and blockers

Up to three: what is at risk, what changed, who can resolve it, and the next action. Link to [[Risks and Assumptions]] and [[Texas Diligence]] as applicable.

## Next priorities

Choose up to three accepted commitments. Record owner, due date, and definition of done in [[Roadmap and Tasks]]; link task IDs here instead of maintaining a competing status list.
