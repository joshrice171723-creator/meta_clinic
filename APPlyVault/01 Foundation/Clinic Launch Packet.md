# Clinic Launch Packet

Home: [[00 Home]] · Offer: [[Customer and Offer]] · Source process: [[MD Pilot]] · Review: [[Texas Diligence]]

## Purpose

Turn Kevin's documented, attorney-assisted cash-pay PLLC startup process into the delivery packet for a **done-for-you launch service for both NPs and PAs in Sherman/North Texas**. Minimize provider input while executing setup through first-patient readiness, including starter SharePoint EHR setup, Kevin's physician support/mentorship, and ongoing support.

**Confirmed:** the founder intends to reuse an existing body of documented work. **Current artifact:** this is an internal packet outline, not the final applicant form or a set of legally reviewed filing documents. Source materials are expected on 2026-08-31 morning and have not yet been received. Do not invent missing legal forms or require completed research to be recreated.

## What “packet” means in this draft

Four connected parts: applicant inputs, documents/resources selected for that applicant, an execution checklist, and evidence for the final handoff. This structure is proposed; confirm whether the intended first version is a document bundle, fillable form, portal, or combination.

## Minimal-input operating design — proposed implementation

Collect essential facts once, prepare the work internally, batch specific approvals/signatures for the applicant, and show only outstanding decisions and blockers. Maintain separate NP and PA applicability paths and locality-specific requirements. Do not require applicants to research the entire startup process themselves.

For every step, distinguish who prepares, who reviews, who authorizes spending, who signs/submits, and what evidence closes it. The provider's decisions, factual attestations, professional judgments, and any required personal actions remain explicit exceptions; “we execute everything” is not permission to invent answers or sign for a client. The final role map requires the actual documents and appropriate review.

Do not use the planning vault as a live applicant database. Keep actual applicant identifiers, financial records, signatures, and confidential attachments in an approved intake system. Use synthetic examples here; collect only what is needed in the eventual intake.

## Source-to-packet inventory

For each existing item, record:

- Item ID, title, author, date/version, and controlled source location.
- What the MD actually did, in what order, and for which clinic/profession/locality.
- Item type: official form, legal advice, checklist, policy, applicant question, workflow, or evidence of completion.
- Whether it can be reused, requires permission, needs a profession/locality variant, or should remain clinic-specific.
- Reviewer, review scope/date, current-source check, and outstanding questions.
- Packet section, fields the applicant must supply, dependencies, and who completes/submits the resulting item.

## Proposed assembly process

1. Receive a permitted, non-patient-data inventory of the MD's materials; preserve original filenames and versions.
2. Map existing work to the sections above and identify true gaps or outdated items.
3. Separate MD-specific, APP-profession-specific, locality-specific, and reusable content; obtain appropriate review.
4. Define applicant questions and document handoffs only from reviewed requirements and actual workflow needs.
5. Test the packet with a synthetic clinic profile; note ambiguity, duplicated inputs, missing dependencies, and manual effort.
6. Run a scoped first-client engagement once the package and required reviews are ready; revise based on actual delivery.

When the materials arrive, index first rather than copying everything into the vault. Sensitive or privileged originals should remain in an approved controlled location.

## Proposed first-patient readiness definition

The engagement's endpoint is an agreed, evidence-backed handoff—not a guarantee of patient volume, profitability, or regulator approval. Determine with the founders and responsible reviewers whether completion requires:

- Relevant formation, professional arrangements, and site/service readiness items evidenced for the specific clinic.
- Agreed business workflows, necessary equipment/supplies, and responsible staff in place.
- If medication supply/pharmacy work is included: activity-specific authority, applicable licenses/registrations, required professional coverage, approved charging/sourcing, storage/records/incident procedures and a synthetic rehearsal evidenced. Unsupported activities remain excluded until separately cleared.
- Starter EHR configured within its reviewed scope, with staff trained and a synthetic intake-to-record-to-follow-up rehearsal completed.
- Open issues categorized as blocking or nonblocking by the appropriate responsible people, not unilaterally by the consultancy.
- Client acknowledgment of the delivered scope, exclusions, ongoing responsibilities, and support/exit path.

Each clinic's actual readiness requirements are to be defined; this is not a completed clearance checklist.
