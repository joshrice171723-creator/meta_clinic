# Business Model

Home: [[00 Home]] · Offer: [[Customer and Offer]] · Validation: [[Customer Discovery]]

## Confirmed business boundary

The consultancy sells clinic-launch help, including a starter SharePoint system. The EHR and inventory-management product will sit in a separate planned entity. Track revenue, delivery cost, support cost, and IP separately; do not assume common ownership, consolidated finances, or automatic revenue sharing. See [[Entity and Product Boundary]].

The founder expressed no deadline pressure. This does not supply a cash budget, unlimited labor, or an approved spending level.

## Revenue hypotheses

- One-time or milestone-based consulting fee for the end-to-end launch project; price and structure undecided.
- Optional post-launch consulting fee, only if a defined need and scope exist.
- EHR/inventory licensing or subscriptions through the separate software entity; standalone purchase, bundle, or reseller arrangement undecided.
- Expert and vendor charges separately identified; do not assume they are included or marked up.

No price, referral arrangement, revenue share, or equity-in-clinic model is approved. Proposed referral compensation, percentage-of-clinic-revenue fees, and financial relationships with clinicians/vendors need case-specific review before use. See L-04 in [[Texas Diligence]].

## Simple economics to maintain

- Project contribution = project revenue − delivery labor − subcontractor costs − project-specific vendor/payment costs.
- Monthly subscription contribution per clinic = monthly revenue − variable infrastructure/vendor costs − attributable support labor.
- Acquisition cost per new customer = attributable sales/marketing expense and labor ÷ new paying customers in the measured cohort.
- Cash runway = available business cash ÷ net monthly cash burn, only when burn is positive and assumptions are explicit.

These are planning definitions, not forecasts. Track cash payment timing separately from revenue and margin. Separate our company's economics from the MD clinic's startup budget and patient-care revenue.

## Commercial decision gates — proposed

Do not scale a service until delivery hours and costs are visible. Assess recurring EHR/inventory economics within the software entity and optional post-launch support within the consultancy; neither should assume the other's revenue or uncompensated labor. Identify any intercompany charges explicitly. Do not build a financial projection by entering unknown inputs as zero.

A pricing experiment should state the exact scope, proposed price, buyer, response, objections, commitment obtained, and delivery-cost estimate. “Interested” is weaker evidence than a scoped paid engagement.
