## Current direction

Meta Clinic is a configurable ambulatory software product for NP- and PA-led cash-pay primary-care and concierge-adjacent clinics in Sherman and North Texas. The current commercial direction is software subscription plus paid FDE onboarding, integration and data migration. A general consultancy or done-for-you clinic-launch service is not part of the current model.

The product is designed to help a clinic operate a connected workflow across patients, scheduling, encounters, charting, cash-pay billing, memberships, inventory and operational worklists. The FDE configures the selected clinic profile and approved integrations, then hands the configured workspace to clinic ownership.

## Customer and operating thesis

The first buyers are expected to be independent NP/PA owners, founding groups or small existing practices that need a focused cash-pay operating system and help moving away from fragmented tools. This is a hypothesis. Local demand has been reported, but the number of qualified buyers, paid commitments, conversion rate and sustainable price remain unknown.

The product must earn its position against configurable platforms such as Tebra. Generic configurability is not enough. The differentiation hypothesis is a focused cash-pay ambulatory model, practical workflow defaults for small NP/PA-led clinics, and an FDE-led path from current systems to a validated operating workspace.

## FDE role

The FDE is an implementation and integration role, not a clinician, legal adviser, billing authority or general administrator. The FDE may configure the clinic, inspect approved source-system exports, map fields, coordinate import/integration work, lead reconciliation and guide staff validation. Access to real patient or clinical data requires separate authorization, agreements, security/privacy review, migration controls and release evidence.

The existing implementation-specialist role is intentionally narrow. Any expansion needed for real-data migration must use a separately reviewed access model; it must not grant broad administrator or clinical authority by assumption.

## Product and entity boundaries

| Subject | Current purpose | Details to settle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meta Clinic software | Subscription product for ambulatory clinic operations | Legal entity, IP, licensing, hosting, support and customer contracts |
| FDE implementation | Configure the clinic and execute approved integration/migration work | Scope, rate card, access, evidence, rollback, change orders and handoff |
| Customer clinic | Owns its operations, users, professional responsibilities and records | Authorized users, data custody, acceptance, clinical accountability and exit |
| Professional services | Clinical, legal, accounting or other regulated work outside the software role | Qualified party, contract, scope, fees and responsibility |
| Guidance and education | Separate mentoring, knowledge and training product if retained | Audience, ownership, packaging and relationship to Meta Clinic |

Do not infer that Meta Clinic owns a clinic, employs its clinicians, exercises clinical authority or provides professional advice merely because an FDE configures software.

## What a solid foundation means

We can describe the buyer, product tier, supported source systems, FDE responsibilities, data-migration boundaries, price hypothesis, support limits, implementation acceptance and the evidence required before live use. We cannot yet claim that every clinical workflow, integration, deployment, security control or professional review is complete.
