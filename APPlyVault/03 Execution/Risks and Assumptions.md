# Risks and Assumptions

Home: [[00 Home]] · Action: [[Roadmap and Tasks]] · Review: [[Texas Diligence]]

Impact ratings are initial judgments, not quantified probabilities. All entries are open, with owners unassigned. A risk is not closed merely because a plan exists.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Assumptions to test

| ID | Hypothesis | Current evidence | What would challenge it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A-01 | APP founders want a coordinated launch service | Founder intent only | Interviews show isolated needs adequately served by existing advisors |
| A-02 | Useful parts of the MD's experience transfer to a selected APP segment | MD project reported; no materials reviewed | Profession/locality differences dominate the proposed playbook |
| A-03 | The SharePoint method offers practical benefits worth packaging | Founder report only | Material clinical gaps, high maintenance burden, poor portability, or no customer preference |
| A-04 | Launch work can become repeatable enough to support a software business | No delivery or unit-economics data | Most work remains bespoke, or software does not reduce effort or improve outcomes |
| A-05 | Clinics will pay for an ongoing service after launch | No retention or recurring-need evidence | Demand ends once the clinic opens |
| A-06 | The company can start with a service that avoids patient-data handling | Proposed design boundary only | Desired deliverables require patient access; scope or review must change |

## Review protocol

At a team review, name the top three risks, new evidence, changed impact, and one next action each. Record closures with evidence, reviewer, date, and residual risk. Record disproved assumptions as learning; do not silently delete them.
