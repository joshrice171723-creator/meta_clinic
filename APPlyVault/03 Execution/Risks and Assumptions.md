**R-13 — High / open:** broad pharmacy configurability is mistaken for universal implemented coverage, every clinic's authority, or a requirement to collect every field. Mitigation: a shared inventory core, explicit capability dependencies, versioned per-clinic/location profiles, separate action permissions and scoped acceptance evidence; unsupported combinations stay disabled. Track implementation under T-13/T-34 and real-use applicability under L-02/L-05/L-06. Owner remains unassigned.

**R-14 — High / open:** an internal medication room, prescriptive authority or “small/cash-pay/our patients only” description is mistaken for authority to dispense or sell drugs, leaving professional coverage, product safety and records inadequate. Mitigation: document the exact activity/site/operator/formulary; obtain scoped classification, supply/charging and staffing findings; validate storage, tracing, controlled/compounding and incident procedures before affected use. Use [[Pharmacy Provider and Counsel Questions]] under T-36 and L-02/L-04/L-05/L-06. No clinic exception or operating pathway has been approved; owner remains unassigned.

**R-17 — High / open:** provisioning/recovery or operator privileges admit the wrong person, revive a disabled account or leave old authority usable. Mitigation/evidence: T-23.1–T-23.4 recipient-bound activation, identity-checked recovery, deactivation races, session/MFA revocation, denied self-grants and protected lifecycle history. **R-18 — High / open:** new content silently alters issued evidence, stale progress appears current, or a paperwork checkbox is treated as completed external work. Mitigation/evidence: T-24.1–T-24.6 version-bound assignment/progress, explicit re-review, evidence-backed transitions and two-client export/restore tests. Detect mismatched version references, missing completion evidence and unowned dependencies; preserve the prior version and stop only the affected release/transition. Source applicability remains R-01/R-10, not a second source-review task. See [Meta Clinic Implementation Tracker](Meta%20Clinic%20Implementation%20Tracker.md). Owner unassigned.

**R-19 — High / open:** clinic screens appear usable while wrong-patient selection, lost edits, stale intake or an unaccepted handoff cause unsafe or falsely completed work. The current board can classify same-day appointment timing but has no durable production check-in event; treating encounter start or a schedule timestamp as arrival would falsely place a patient in the waiting room. Mitigation/evidence: T-13.1–T-13.4 observed synthetic workflow, identity/visit binding, authorship/version rules, explicit check-in/ownership events and interrupted-session/conflict prototypes. Signals include missing owner, mismatched identity/version and unacknowledged save; pause the affected transition and use the reviewed fallback rather than inventing clinical behavior. Theme completion and schedule-aware presentation are not workflow validation. See [Meta Clinic Implementation Tracker](Meta%20Clinic%20Implementation%20Tracker.md). Owner unassigned.

**R-20 — High / open:** a checkout return, stale provider event, mismatched amount, duplicate delivery, or email failure could falsely activate a membership or leave a paid customer without onboarding access. Current mitigation is test-mode-only checkout; signed-event and fresh-provider reconciliation; clinic/account/amount/currency/fee correlation; idempotent patient, ledger, receipt and task creation; serialized receipt delivery; and an explicit pending-email signal in the clinic pipeline. Production requires live-provider custody, monitored retry and reconciliation, external email deliverability, duplicate-identity recovery, privacy/legal review and hosted acceptance before real use. Owner unassigned.

## Review protocol

At a team review, name the top three risks, new evidence, changed impact, and one next action each. Record closures with evidence, reviewer, date, and residual risk. Record disproved assumptions as learning; do not silently delete them.

Use [[Working Agreement#Failure-first planning and review]] to turn a risk into a concrete trigger → harm → detection → guard/test → recovery/stop decision. Consider unavailable people, uncertain demand, underestimated labor and unauthorized reuse as well as software faults (R-04/R-05/R-06/R-11). A hypothetical failure is not an incident; a passed mock is not hosted evidence. Route the next action into one bounded Story under the appropriate Epic in the [Meta Clinic Implementation Tracker](Meta%20Clinic%20Implementation%20Tracker.md) and record material results through [[Evidence Note Template]].
