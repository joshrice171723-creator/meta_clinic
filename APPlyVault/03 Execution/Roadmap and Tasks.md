## Rules

This is the canonical task register. All future work below is proposed until a person accepts ownership and timing. **No due dates, budgets, outreach, professional engagements, or product builds are committed.** The MD's independent research is reported in progress; it is not represented as completed company diligence.

Statuses: backlog, ready, in progress, awaiting input, blocked, done. “Done” requires a linked deliverable or recorded result. Blocked items name the dependency and next action. Unknown owners/dates stay unassigned / TBD.

## Milestones and evidence gates — proposed

| Gate | Outcome | Evidence required to pass |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G0 — Agreed foundation | We know what we are trying to sell and to whom | First segment/outcome, roles, constraints, boundaries, and validation plan recorded in [[Decisions]] |
| G1 — Grounded offer | A specific offer is worth testing | MD learnings with transfer limits; target-customer evidence; scoped offer and cost/price hypothesis; critical review needs identified |
| G2 — Ready for a paid pilot | We can responsibly deliver the agreed scope | Applicable professional review, contract/scope, accepted owner/capacity, measurable outcome, and data boundary; unresolved blocking issues closed or scope narrowed |
| G3 — Repeatable delivery / product choice | We know what should become reusable service or software | Actual paid delivery results, labor/rework/costs, recurring need evidence, approved reuse rights, and build / configure / partner / defer decision |

Passing a company milestone is not a clinical go-live authorization. Customer clinics need their own applicable readiness evidence and responsible professionals.
