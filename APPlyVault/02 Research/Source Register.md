## How to use this register

These are primary-source starting references, not a completed legal or security review. “Accessed” means the page was reviewed on that date, not that every statement is current controlling law. Recheck applicability and effective dates before relying on a source for a client. Resolve conflicting or outdated guidance with current authority and a qualified reviewer.

D-042 records the bounded staging setup, without a vulnerability waiver. S-90 is [Let's Encrypt's Certbot IP-certificate guidance](https://letsencrypt.org/2026/03/11/shorter-certs-certbot): public-trust IP certificates, short lifetime, webroot validation and renewal/reload requirements. S-91 is the scoped [Debian glibc](https://security-tracker.debian.org/tracker/CVE-2026-5450), [Perl](https://security-tracker.debian.org/tracker/CVE-2026-57433), [libssh2](https://security-tracker.debian.org/tracker/CVE-2026-66033), [SQLite](https://security-tracker.debian.org/tracker/CVE-2026-11824) and [OpenSSL](https://mirror.openssl-library.org/news/vulnerabilities-3.5/) advisory review, read alongside then-current ECR counts and source/configuration. These establish bounded historical background, not current hosting evidence or a passed release gate. The former review-access runbook is retired from active source.
