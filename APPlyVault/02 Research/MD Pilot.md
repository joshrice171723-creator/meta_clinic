## Evidence capture

Use [[Evidence Note Template]] for each meaningful lesson. Record the actual event date, profession, locality, clinic services, source, time/cost, dependency, resolution, and reviewer. Distinguish elapsed waiting time from labor time.

Label each lesson as:

- **Candidate reusable:** potentially useful across clinics, still requires validation.
- **Profession-specific:** MD, NP/APRN, or PA requirements may differ.
- **Locality-specific:** tied to this site or authority.
- **Clinic/service-specific:** depends on the care model, payer, or equipment.
- **One-off / unresolved:** insufficient evidence to generalize.
