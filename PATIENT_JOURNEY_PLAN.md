Keep simultaneous clinical, billing and contact work individually visible, owned and recoverable after encounter closure. The platform remains multi-specialty; weight loss is the first configured example, not a hardcoded universal clinical protocol.

Do not treat the earlier comparison's **65%/90% estimates**, dated source observations or old “next action” as a current completion claim. Use the tracker rows and the case-level evidence below. ## Governing choices

- **D-078 / CF-01–04:** one shared core with specialty, modality, payment and operational capabilities chosen during setup. The weight-loss starter changes actual visit types, versioned intake, available components and follow-up context. Finalization and operation-specific clinic-managed, Meta Clinic-managed or FDE-assisted changes preserve prior records and unfinished work. A new clinic is not a renamed existing clinic; a label alone is not workflow proof.
- **D-074 / EN-01–09:** only public membership account creation has the 18+ gate, in UI and backend. Invalid/future birth dates and ages 150+ are rejected; staff-created newborn records remain valid. Never-member unpaid prospects and former inactive members cannot self-book online; callback and authorized staff/walk-in work remain available. Verified server payment evidence, not a browser return, converts an account once. Pending cancellation does not by itself end a still-effective membership.
- **D-073 / OP rows:** a missing prerequisite blocks the specific dependent action, not the entire patient. Ownership, coverage, blocker, resolver and independent available work remain legible. Acceptance pairs a denied action with a permitted one in the same relevant context; wrong role/clinic, stale revision, retry and uncertain external outcomes remain explicit.
- **D-072 and existing domain owners:** prepare local vendor-dependent work through existing source-owned records and backend simulation. Prescription preparation is not transmission or dispensing; lab order is not result, review or contact; stock reservation is not consumption; invoice or redirect is not paid evidence. Do not auto-send prepared backlog when a connection appears.
- **Clinical and data boundaries:** clinic-selected medicines, lab panels, treatment prerequisites, urgency, follow-up timing and coverage destinations require their own sourced/configured decisions. U.S. healthcare references guide substantive field design; the [archived source register](records/superseded-plans/PATIENT_JOURNEY_EXECUTION_PLAN.md#source-to-workflow-reference-register) records dated applicability, and the [standards procedure](docs/development/healthcare-standards.md) governs new changes. No FHIR conformance, clinical recommendation or legal approval follows from these mappings.
