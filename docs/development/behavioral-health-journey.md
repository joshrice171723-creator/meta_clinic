### Field/control and recovery contract

Current intake mechanics: backend `PrevisitIntake`/`BehavioralHealthQuestionnaire`, encrypted append-only `PrevisitNote`; staff writes require intake permission, patient writes their authorized portal context. All values below are entered, never calculated clinical findings. Every saved response retains form ID/version, question identity, answer state, recorder/time and revision; reuse does not attest current accuracy. Initial/follow-up labels differ only where declared by the form. Common answer states are `answered`, `unanswered`, `not_asked`, `unknown`, `declined`, `not_applicable`, `negative`; only `answered` may carry a value. Draft permits missing answers; group completion follows `NurseWorkflow`, not an invented clinical gate. Earlier answers become historical at the next visit and require explicit current confirmation before clinical reliance.
